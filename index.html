NAS Google Drive Import

NAS Google Drive Import is a private utility for transferring photos and videos from a user's Google Drive to their privately operated NAS and Immich photo library.

How it works

The application connects to Google Drive with read-only access. It searches for supported photo and video files and allows the authenticated user to import those files into their own private NAS storage.

The application does not modify or delete files stored in Google Drive.

Data access

Google Drive access is used exclusively to read files selected for transfer to the user's private NAS environment.

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
