Privacy Policy

NAS Google Drive Import

NAS Google Drive Import is a private utility that allows an authenticated user to transfer photos and videos from their Google Drive account to their privately operated NAS and Immich photo library.

Google Drive access

The application requests read-only access to Google Drive. This access is used solely to identify and download supported photo and video files for transfer to the user's private NAS.

The application does not modify or delete files in Google Drive.

Use of Google user data

Google user data accessed by the application is used only to provide the requested file-import functionality.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Storage

Imported media files are stored on the user's privately operated NAS and Immich installation.

Authentication credentials and OAuth tokens required for the Google Drive connection are stored locally on the NAS and are not intentionally transmitted to third parties other than Google as required for authentication and API access.

Data retention and deletion

Imported files remain under the control of the NAS owner. The user can remove imported media from their NAS or Immich installation at any time.

Google Drive authorization can be revoked through the user's Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy can be sent to: nas-drive-import@web.de
